Privacy first

Patient data never leaves your phone

Your logbook is locked with your PIN and encrypted on the device. The community only ever sees what you choose to post, and the app helps you remove identifiers first.

  • 🔐AES-256 encryption on your phone, plus an encrypted backup in your own Google Drive.
  • 🫥De-identification tools and identifier warnings before anything is posted.
  • 🛡️Approved members only, with reporting and admin moderation.
  • 📴Works offline in the ward and OPD.
